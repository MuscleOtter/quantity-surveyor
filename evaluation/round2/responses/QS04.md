# Freestanding project completeness review — QS04

**This is a partial concept scope, not a complete project budget.** The supplied inputs contain no package values; neither an included subtotal nor a complete total can be calculated. Unknown work remains unpriced rather than zero. Basis: single-storey, 1,250 m² gross internal area on a 6,000 m² undeveloped synthetic Arizona site; target opening 14 months after acquisition.

| Status | Scope / evidence in supplied brief | Urgent question and accountable role |
|---|---|---|
| Included | Retail interior finishes; display millwork; store lighting | Interior design/package leads: define quantities, specification, accessibility interfaces and pricing boundary |
| Included | Above-ground steel frame; facade and roof cladding | Structural/envelope leads: confirm foundations excluded from frame, roof build-up/supports, weatherproofing, testing and access boundaries |
| Included | In-building HVAC distribution | MEP lead: separate distribution from central plant, incoming power, controls and commissioning |
| Excluded | Land purchase; merchandise inventory | Owner: maintain these exclusions separately from development budget |
| Unknown | Survey/levels, geotechnical investigation, enabling works, earthworks, temporary works and substructure | Owner/civil/structural leads: commission site and ground information; establish grading, foundation, dewatering and site-access scope |
| Unknown | Incoming utilities capacity, connections/diversions, external drainage and attenuation | Civil/MEP leads: obtain provider capacity letters, connection requirements, drainage strategy and approval/programme evidence |
| Unknown | Parking, landscaping, roads/access and external lighting | Civil/landscape/electrical leads: define site boundary, lighting, parking, accessible routes and responsibility |
| Unknown | Fire-water supply and wider fire/life-safety systems | Fire/MEP leads: develop fire strategy and confirm water capacity, protection systems and provider/authority interfaces |
| Unknown | HVAC central plant; remaining electrical/plumbing systems, controls, security/IT and specialist equipment | MEP/owner leads: identify whole-building systems, performance requirements and direct-purchase interfaces; distribution alone does not establish complete services |
| Unknown | Testing, commissioning and handover | Design/construction leads: assign integrated testing, inspections, manuals, training and completion responsibility |
| Unknown | Professional fees; authority fees; utility fees; planning and building/accessibility/authority review | Owner/design lead: establish local authority, approval route, fee basis and appointment scope; no planning decision or project-specific authority requirements have been verified |
| Unknown | Contractor preliminaries and overhead/profit | Cost/procurement lead: establish programme/logistics pricing and percentage bases; confirm what package prices already include |
| Unknown | Contingency, escalation and tax | Owner/cost lead: define price date, tax basis, evidenced risk exposure and addition bases |
| Unknown | Other owner direct costs, loose furniture/equipment, financing boundary | Owner: define complete owner-budget boundary and allocate each item once |

Priorities: (1) owner commissions site survey, ground investigation and provider capacity enquiries; (2) design leads establish planning, fire, energy and accessibility requirements with the actual authorities/providers; (3) cost lead assembles a responsibility/pricing matrix and programme, tests long-lead approvals and procurement against the 14-month target, then prices supported packages.

Source: QS04 project, included_packages, explicit_exclusions, unclear_packages and brief. No surveyed levels, geotechnical report, utility capacity letters, energy model, fire strategy or planning decision were provided. No official regulatory checks were performed; the synthetic location lacks a specified local authority. The area ratio 1,250/6,000 = 20.83% is merely an input relationship, not verified site coverage or permission evidence.
