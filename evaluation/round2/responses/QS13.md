NYC identifies New Building applications for new structures. A New York State registered architect or professional engineer must file the construction plans. The usual route includes DOB examination for legal/zoning objections; professional certification can replace plan examination, but requires legal compliance and remains subject to audit. NRM cannot substitute for either route. [NYC DOB permits](https://www.nyc.gov/site/buildings/property-or-business-owner/obtaining-a-permit.page).

Engage a local registered design professional to establish the site-specific zoning, building/fire/accessibility and permit strategy, utility approvals, inspections and occupancy requirements. Obtain the missing zoning analysis, geotechnical report and fire strategy; concept drawings and an area schedule cannot establish clearance. DOB describes owner responsibility and new-building sign-offs/Certificate of Occupancy. [NYC owner requirements](https://www.nyc.gov/site/buildings/property-or-business-owner/project-requirements-owner.page).
