The inputs supply cash paid, not invoice or accrued-cost totals. An unpaid invoice could be incurred cost without being paid; it would sit within the relevant package forecast rather than become an automatic extra cost. Before using this as an incurred-cost forecast, reconcile invoices, accruals and payments. On the supplied paid-cash basis, the remaining figures include all forecast amounts not yet paid.

Residual contingency is additional and excludes identified overruns already within package forecast finals. No commitment is inferred for owner costs. Both forecast constructions were executed and reconciled.
