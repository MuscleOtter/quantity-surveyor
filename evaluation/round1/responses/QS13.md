No. NRM is a measurement and cost-planning convention; it does not remove NYC regulatory obligations. RICS describes NRM 1 for capital cost planning and NRM 2 for detailed measurement, with the 2021 documents reissued as practice information in 2022. Agree the appropriate method and edition in the appointment/contract; no measurement clause has been supplied. [RICS NRM](https://www.rics.org/profession-standards/rics-standards-and-guidance/sector-standards/construction-standards/nrm).

RICS's separate global Cost prediction professional standard has mandatory requirements for RICS professionals and regulated firms. The consultant’s status needs confirmation; that obligation does not make NRM mandatory everywhere. [RICS Cost prediction](https://www.rics.org/profession-standards/rics-standards-and-guidance/sector-standards/construction-standards/rics-cost-prediction-professional-statement-global-1st-edition).
