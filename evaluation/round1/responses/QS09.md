Each month's retention deduction is the lesser of 5% of gross valuation and the unused USD 40,000 cap. The cap is reached in month 3, so month 4's valuation has no new retention deduction. After month 4's valuation, 50% of accumulated retention, USD 20,000, is released; the other USD 20,000 is released in month 10.

Advance recovery is 10% of each gross valuation, capped at total recovery of USD 100,000: 20,000 + 30,000 + 30,000 + 20,000. No retention or advance recovery is applied to retention releases or to the initial advance.

Gross-to-cash reconciliation: 1,000,000 gross − 40,000 retention withheld − 100,000 advance recovered = 860,000 net valuation receipts. Add 100,000 month 0 advance and 40,000 retention releases = 1,000,000 final receipts. The recoverable advance changes timing and adds no net contract cost. No other cash movement is supplied for months 5–9. Monthly balances and terminal conservation were executed and checked against the fictional contract terms.
