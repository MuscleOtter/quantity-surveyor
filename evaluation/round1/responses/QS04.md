# Scope completeness review

**This is a partial package scope, not a complete project budget.** No costs are provided, so neither a priced subtotal nor a complete total can be calculated. At concept stage, the undeveloped 6,000 m² site and 1,250 m² single-storey building require site, whole-building and owner-cost coverage beyond the listed retail and above-ground packages. The 14-month opening target remains untested.

Prioritise site/ground information, utility and fire-water feasibility, and the planning/approval path: these can change scope and the opening date. Then coordinate the whole-building scope matrix with the designers and price the gaps from actual specifications and quotations. The project manager should test the 14-month target against approvals, design, utility lead times, construction and commissioning.

No Arizona code, planning entitlement, tax rate or approval duration has been verified; no regulatory conclusion is made from the synthetic location. Obtain the actual site jurisdiction and authority evidence when the design and cost decision depends on them. The review uses only the supplied package list and evidence gaps; listed documents have not been inspected because none were provided.
